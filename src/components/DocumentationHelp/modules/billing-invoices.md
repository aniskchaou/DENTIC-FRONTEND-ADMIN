# Billing & Invoices Module

The Billing & Invoices module enables clinics to efficiently manage financial transactions, generate invoices, and track payments for patient services. Users can create detailed bills by selecting a patient, clinic, and services provided, specifying quantities, applying discounts, taxes, and payment methods. The module supports a wide range of dental services, each with customizable pricing.

As users add services to a bill, the module automatically calculates totals, allowing for real-time adjustments and review before finalizing the invoice. Once ready, invoices can be generated, saved, and previewed in a dialog. The system also supports downloading invoices as PDF documents for sharing or record-keeping.

A comprehensive invoice data table displays all invoices, including patient and clinic information, total amounts, payment status, dates, and actions for viewing or downloading. Users can filter, sort, and export invoice data, making it easy to manage large volumes of transactions.

Additional features include support for multiple payment methods (cash, card, insurance), due dates, notes, and itemized billing. Administrators can monitor outstanding payments, track financial performance, and ensure accurate billing practices.

Overall, the Billing & Invoices module streamlines the financial workflow of the clinic, reduces administrative burden, and ensures transparency and accuracy in all billing operations.
