# Payments Module 

The Payments module provides clinics with a comprehensive system for managing and tracking all financial transactions related to patient services. Users can view a list of payments in a sortable and filterable data grid, displaying details such as patient name, clinic, amount, currency, payment method, payment status, and payment date. The module supports adding new payments, viewing payment details, and deleting payments, either individually or in bulk.

To add a new payment, users can open the creation dialog, select the patient and clinic, enter the payment amount, choose the payment method (such as cash, card, or insurance), and specify the payment status. Payment records are automatically updated and reflected in the data grid, ensuring accurate and up-to-date financial tracking.

The module provides quick access to payment actions through a contextual menu, allowing users to view detailed payment information or delete records with confirmation prompts. Payment details include all relevant information and are displayed in a clear, organized format for easy review.

Visual widgets and charts can be enabled to summarize payment statistics, such as total payments, payment distribution by method, and trends over time. Integration with patient and clinic records ensures that payments are accurately linked and easily accessible.

Bulk actions, such as deleting multiple payments, are supported to streamline administrative tasks. The module also provides notifications and feedback for all major actions, such as creation, update, deletion, and viewing details.

Overall, the Payments module centralizes financial management, supports efficient administration, and ensures transparency and accuracy in all payment operations within
