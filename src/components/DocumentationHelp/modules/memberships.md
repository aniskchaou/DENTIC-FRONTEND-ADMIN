# Memberships Module 

The Memberships module enables clinics to manage patient membership plans, track benefits, payment status, and renewal dates in a centralized and user-friendly interface. Users can view all memberships in a sortable and filterable data grid, displaying details such as plan type (Premium, Standard, Basic), payment status (Active, Inactive, Expired, Cancelled), benefits, validity period, renewal date, last payment amount, and last payment date.

Adding a new membership is straightforward—users can open the creation dialog, select a plan, specify benefits, set validity dates, and enter payment details. Editing and deleting memberships is supported, with confirmation prompts to prevent accidental changes. Bulk actions and quick refresh options help keep membership records up to date.

Each membership record can be viewed in detail, showing all associated benefits, status, payment history, and important dates. Color-coded chips and icons make it easy to identify plan types and payment statuses at a glance. The module also supports exporting membership data for reporting and analysis.

Integration with patient records ensures that memberships are accurately linked, and administrators can monitor the status and renewal of plans to maintain patient engagement. The module provides notifications and feedback for all major actions, such as creation, update, deletion, and viewing details.

Overall, the Memberships module streamlines the management of patient plans, supports efficient administration, and helps clinics deliver enhanced value to their patients.
