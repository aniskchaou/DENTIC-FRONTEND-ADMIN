# Clinic Management Module

The Clinic Management module provides a centralized interface for managing all clinic locations and their essential details. Users can view a list of clinics in a sortable and filterable data grid, displaying information such as clinic name, address, city, state, zip code, country, contact number, email, and working hours. Each clinic entry can be selected for viewing, editing, or deletion.

To add a new clinic, users can open the "Add Clinic" dialog and fill out the required fields, including contact information and operational hours. The module supports editing existing clinics, allowing administrators to update details as needed. All changes are reflected in real-time, ensuring that staff always have access to the latest information.

The module also provides a detailed view for each clinic, showing general information, contact details, working hours for each day, and timestamps for creation and last update. This helps administrators maintain accurate records and quickly access important data.

Bulk actions, such as deleting clinics, are supported with confirmation prompts to prevent accidental data loss. The module integrates with other parts of the system, such as doctor availability and appointment scheduling, ensuring seamless clinic operations.

Overall, the Clinic Management module streamlines the process of adding, updating, and organizing clinic information, supporting efficient administration and high-quality patient care across multiple locations.
