# Patient Management Module

The Patient Management module provides clinics with a comprehensive system for handling all aspects of patient records and interactions. Users can view, add, edit, and delete patient profiles, each containing essential information such as full name, medical record number, blood type, allergies, and medical history. The module supports advanced filtering and searching, making it easy to locate specific patients or groups based on various criteria.

Patient records are displayed in a sortable and filterable data grid, with interactive chips and color-coded indicators for attributes like blood type and allergies. Bulk actions are available for removing or updating multiple patients at once, and users can export or print patient data for documentation or sharing. The module also supports duplicating patient records and sharing information via the Web Share API.

Integrated AI-powered tools allow users to generate health profile summaries and risk stratification reports for individual patients, providing valuable insights into patient health and potential risks. These features analyze medical history, appointments, imaging findings, and other data to assist clinicians in decision-making.

The module includes widgets and charts that summarize patient statistics, such as total patients and distribution by blood type. Visualizations help administrators monitor clinic demographics and trends over time.

Patient profiles are linked to appointments, treatments, and other clinic modules, ensuring seamless workflow and accurate record-keeping. The module provides notifications and feedback for all major actions, such as creation, update, deletion, and export.

Overall, the Patient Management module centralizes patient data, supports efficient administration, and enhances the quality of care delivered by the clinic.
