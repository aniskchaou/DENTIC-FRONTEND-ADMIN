# Odontogram Module 

The Odontogram module provides dental professionals with an advanced tool for visualizing, recording, and managing the condition and treatment history of each tooth for every patient. The module displays a detailed dental chart, representing all 32 teeth, with color-coded indicators for statuses such as healthy, decayed, filled, or missing. Each tooth can be selected to view or update its status, add notes, record procedures, operations, diagnoses, and summarize treatments.

Users can filter the odontogram by patient, doctor, clinic, appointment, and membership, ensuring that the correct dental records are accessed and updated. The module supports uploading images, marking affected tooth surfaces, and maintaining a comprehensive history of all changes and treatments for each tooth. All updates are saved both locally and to the backend, ensuring data consistency and security.

Advanced features include AI-powered condition prediction, which analyzes the current odontogram and forecasts potential dental issues, and treatment simulation, which allows users to model the effects of proposed treatment plans on the dental chart. The module also offers a 3D jaw visualization, providing a realistic view of tooth positions and statuses.

Bulk export and download options are available, enabling users to save the odontogram as an image for documentation or sharing. The module integrates seamlessly with other clinic records, such as appointments and memberships, and provides detailed tables for viewing the treatment history of individual teeth or all teeth for a patient.

Overall, the Odontogram module centralizes dental charting, supports clinical decision-making, and enhances the quality and accuracy of dental record-keeping within the clinic.
