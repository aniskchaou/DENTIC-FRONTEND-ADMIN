# Doctor Management Module

The Doctor Management module enables clinics to efficiently organize and maintain comprehensive profiles for all doctors. Users can view a list of doctors with key details such as name, license number, specialization, years of experience, consultation fee, available days, and ratings. The module supports adding new doctors, editing existing profiles, and removing doctors who are no longer part of the clinic.

When creating or editing a doctor profile, administrators can input personal information, professional credentials, areas of specialization, and set working schedules. The module allows for the assignment of specific time slots and days of availability, ensuring accurate appointment booking and resource planning.

Doctor profiles are linked to appointments, prescriptions, and patient records, providing seamless integration across the clinic’s workflow. The module also features charts and analytics, such as average rating, experience trends, and distribution by specialty, helping administrators monitor performance and optimize staffing.

Bulk actions, such as deleting or updating multiple profiles, are supported for efficient management. The module includes tools for generating license numbers and matching patient cases to the most suitable doctor using AI-powered suggestions.

Overall, the Doctor Management module streamlines the process of maintaining doctor information, supports effective scheduling, and enhances the quality of care provided by the clinic.
