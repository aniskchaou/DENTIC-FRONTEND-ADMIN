# Staff Management Module

The Staff Management module provides clinics with a comprehensive system for organizing, monitoring, and maintaining staff profiles and roles. Users can view all staff members in a sortable and filterable data grid, displaying details such as full name, role, email, phone number, and status (Active, On Leave, Inactive). Color-coded chips make it easy to identify staff status at a glance.

Administrators can add new staff members by entering personal and professional details, assign roles, and set contact information. Editing and deleting staff profiles is supported, with confirmation prompts to prevent accidental changes. Bulk actions and quick refresh options help keep staff records up to date.

The module features interactive charts, including doughnut, bar, and line charts, to visualize staff distribution by role and monitor status trends. Widgets summarize total staff, active staff, staff on leave, and inactive staff, providing a quick overview of clinic workforce.

Each staff profile can be viewed in detail, showing all associated information and allowing for edits as needed. Integration with other modules ensures that staff records are accurately linked to appointments, clinic locations, and operational workflows.
