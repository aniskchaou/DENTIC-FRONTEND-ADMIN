# Patient Analytics 

The Patient Analytics module provides clinics with powerful tools to visualize, analyze, and understand patient data through interactive charts and statistics. Upon accessing the module, users are presented with a variety of chart types, including bar, pie, doughnut, line, polar area, radar, scatter, and bubble charts, each offering unique insights into patient demographics and clinic activity.

Key analytics include insurance distribution, blood type breakdown, city-wise patient counts, monthly registration trends, payment status, allergy prevalence, age versus amount correlations, and gender distribution. Each chart is dynamically generated from real patient data, allowing administrators and clinicians to monitor trends, identify patterns, and make informed decisions.

The module supports both demo and live data modes, automatically fetching and preparing charts based on the current environment. Users can filter and explore data interactively, gaining insights into patient profiles, health risks, and clinic performance.

Overall, the Patient Analytics module centralizes patient statistics, enhances data-driven decision-making, and helps clinics optimize care delivery and resource allocation.

# Medicament Analytics 

The Medicament Analytics module enables clinics to visualize and analyze medicament inventory and usage through a suite of interactive charts. Users can explore data by category, manufacturer, type, stock status, active ingredients, price, and monthly additions, using bar, pie, doughnut, line, polar area, radar, scatter, and bubble charts.

Key features include tracking the number of medicaments by category and manufacturer, monitoring stock status and active ingredient distribution, analyzing price versus stock levels, and viewing trends in medicament additions over time. The module supports both demo and live data, automatically updating charts based on the latest inventory information.

Administrators can use these analytics to optimize inventory management, identify popular or low-stock items, and ensure efficient procurement and usage of medicaments. The module also supports exporting data for reporting and further analysis.
