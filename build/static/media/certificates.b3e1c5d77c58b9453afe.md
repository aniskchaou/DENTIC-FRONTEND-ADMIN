# Certificates Module

The Certificates module allows clinics to create, manage, and issue medical certificates for patients in a streamlined and secure manner. Users can view all certificates in a sortable and filterable table, with details such as certificate number, type, patient, doctor, clinic, status, issue date, validity period, recommendations, and notes. Advanced filtering options make it easy to find certificates by type, status, patient, doctor, clinic, or date range.

Certificates can be created and edited directly from the interface, with support for adding diagnosis, treatment details, recommendations, and notes. The module enables bulk actions, such as deleting or updating the status of multiple certificates at once, improving administrative efficiency.

Visual charts display certificate statistics by type and status, helping administrators monitor certificate activity and trends. Users can export certificate data to Excel for reporting or print certificates as PDF documents for official use. Each certificate can be previewed, downloaded, or shared, and includes a signature area for the attending doctor.

The module integrates with patient, doctor, and clinic records, ensuring that certificates are accurately linked to relevant individuals and locations. Notifications and feedback are provided for all major actions, such as creation, update, deletion, and export.

Overall, the Certificates module simplifies the process of issuing and managing medical certificates, enhances record-keeping, and supports compliance with regulatory requirements within the clinic.
